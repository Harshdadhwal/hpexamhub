🎓 Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

✔ Graduate in any discipline from a recognized University.
✔ Candidate should have passed Matriculation & 10+2 from a school/institution situated in Himachal Pradesh (except Bonafide Himachalis).

Experience Required

PACS Quota: Minimum 3 years continuous service as Secretary (as per HP Cooperative Societies Rules, 1971) of Primary Agriculture Credit Society.
OCS Quota: Minimum 5 years continuous service in a cooperative society (Other Societies or PACS employees not covered as Trained Secretaries).

Age Limit (as on 01.01.2026)

Above 18 years & below 45 years. Born between 02.01.1981 and 01.01.2008 (both inclusive).
Relaxations: SC/ST/OBC/Physically Handicapped/WFF – 5 years; Ex-Servicemen up to 12 years max as per norms.

✅ Candidates must be bonafide Himachalis for reserved category benefits. OBC creamy layer not eligible. No defaulter of society.

📋 How to Apply Online

  1. Visit HPSCB official website: www.hpscb.bank.in
  2. Click on “Recruitment Link” → “APPLY ONLINE” for Junior Clerk (PACS/OCS).
  3. Complete new registration to get Provisional Registration Number & Password.
  4. Fill application form carefully, upload scanned photo (4.5x3.5cm), signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration as per specifications.
  5. Pay application fee through online gateway (Debit/Credit/Net Banking).
  6. Preview and submit before 29/05/2026. Take printout of e-receipt & final form.
  7. Keep email/mobile active for call letter download.
Note: Signature must be in running handwriting (not capital letters). Left thumb impression clear. Handwritten declaration must be in candidate’s own handwriting (English).
